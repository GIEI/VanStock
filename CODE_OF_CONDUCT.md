# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and open to feedback.
- Focus discussion on ideas, code, and project outcomes.
- Respect privacy. Do not share personal, customer, operational, or security
  information without explicit permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
disclosure of confidential or security-sensitive information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to a repository maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers
may remove content, limit participation, or take other appropriate action.
